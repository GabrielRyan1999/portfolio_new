# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, React Router

## Users
Recruiters, freelance clients, and students. A broad audience evaluating technical capability, educational expertise, or seeking mentorship.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a personal showcase and portfolio for Gabriel Ryan, aimed at securing freelance projects and displaying a gallery of past work, technical case studies, and classroom/educational experience.

## Positioning
An intersection of technology and education. Gabriel is an IT professional/developer and an educator, offering a unique blend of custom web platform development, edtech solutions, curriculum development, and tech mentoring.

## Operating Context
Viewed on web browsers (desktop and mobile), typically accessed via direct links from social media profiles (LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub) or direct outreach.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fully responsive Single Page Application (SPA) architecture with React Router.
- Uses Formspree for the contact form functionality.
- Implements a global light/dark mode toggle.
- Uses Framer Motion for scroll animations and page transitions.

## Brand Commitments
- Branding balances the identities of "IT Developer" and "Educator".
- The aesthetic must be creative, approachable, and engaging. It should explicitly avoid a strict, stiff, or overly corporate/traditional professional look.

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing portfolio architecture with defined sections: Selected Work, Classroom gallery, Case Studies, Service offerings, Experience timeline, and Testimonials.
- Functional floating dock navigation and global footer.

## Product Principles
- **Approachable Expertise**: Demonstrate technical depth while maintaining the welcoming, accessible tone of an educator.
- **Dynamic Showcase**: Utilize purposeful motion and interactivity (Framer Motion) to bring past projects and teaching experiences to life without overwhelming the user.
- **Audience-Agnostic Navigation**: Ensure that diverse visitors (clients, recruiters, students) can easily navigate to the content most relevant to them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Must maintain readable typography and sufficient color contrast across both light and dark modes, ensuring an inclusive experience for all visitors.
